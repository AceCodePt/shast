The `root` container is 600px wide with `display: flex`. The two children have `flex: 1 1 0%` and `flex: 2 1 0%` respectively, which means they share the available space in proportion to their flex-grow values (1:2 ratio).

Since there's no gap or padding involved, the 600px is divided between the two children in a 1:2 ratio:
- `root>a` gets 1/(1+2) × 600 = 200px
