## Model reply

The root element has a height of 900px (exceeding the 300px viewport height), allowing vertical scrolling to bring `root>lower` (located at y=700) into view. However, `root>sideways` extends horizontally to 900px width while `root` has `overflow: visible`, which does not generate horizontal scrollbars or enable horizontal scrolling. Thus, `root>sideways` cannot be reached by ordinary scrolling.

ANSWER: root>sideways
