## Model reply

Adding `position: relative` to `root>plain` makes it the nearest positioned ancestor and containing block for the absolute-positioned `root>plain>pin`. An absolute element's containing block origin corresponds to the inner edge of its ancestor's padding box, which for `root>plain` is at `x = 20 + 30 = 50` and `y = 20 + 30 = 50`. Adding the pin's `left: 10px` and `top: 10px` offsets places its top-left corner at x=60, y=60.
