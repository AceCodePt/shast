## Reasoning

Currently, `root>plain>pin` has `position: absolute` but its parent `root>plain` has `position: static`. This means `pin` is positioned relative to `root` (the nearest positioned ancestor), placing it at x=10, y=10 relative to `root`'s origin.

When `position: relative` is added to `root>plain`, it becomes the containing block for `pin`. The `left: 10px` and `top: 10px` values will now be calculated relative to `root>plain`'s padding box instead.

`root>plain` renders at x=20, y=20 in the viewport. With `pin` offset by 10px from that origin, the new position becomes 20+10=30 for both coordinates.

ANSWER: 30,30
