2. `root>leftHalf` is absolutely positioned at x=0, y=0 with width=70px and a dark background (rgb(16,16,16))
3. `root>rightHalf` is absolutely positioned at x=60, y=0 with width=80px and a dark background (rgb(32,32,32))

The text "Hidden by two" would start in this covered region. The screenshot confirms this - it shows mostly white space with two dark rectangles in the top-left corner where the text would be.
