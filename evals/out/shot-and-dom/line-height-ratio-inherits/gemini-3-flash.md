## Question

`root` declares `font-size: 16px` and `line-height: 2`. `root>big` declares `font-size: 32px` and nothing else. How tall does `root>big` render?

## Model reply

The `root` element declares a unitless `line-height: 2`, which is inherited by its child `root>big` as a ratio rather than a fixed pixel value. Consequently, `root>big` calculates its own line height by multiplying its `font-size` (32px) by this ratio (2), resulting in 64px. The provided browser data confirms that `root>big` has a computed `line-height` of 64px and a bounding box height of 64px.

ANSWER: 64
