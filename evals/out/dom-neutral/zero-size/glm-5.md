## Reasoning

`root>ghost` has `display: block` (not `none`), `overflow: visible`, and `opacity: 1`. Its box collapses to 0×0, but with `overflow: visible` the text content overflows the zero-size box and is still painted by the browser — it is not clipped. The "Important warning" text would render starting at the box's origin (x=0, y=0), overlapping with the "Everything is fine" text from `root>visible`, which occupies the same position. Nothing in the computed style (`visibility` defaults to `visible`, `opacity` is 1, `color` is black) suppresses painting.
