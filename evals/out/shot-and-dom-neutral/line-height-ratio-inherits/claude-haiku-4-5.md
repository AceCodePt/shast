## Model reply

Looking at the data provided:

The `root` element has `font-size: 16px` and `line-height: 2` (a unitless ratio). This means the root's line-height is `2 × 16px = 32px`.

The `root>big` element declares `font-size: 32px` and inherits from its parent. Since `root>big` doesn't explicitly set `line-height`, it inherits the line-height value from `root`. However, when a line-height is specified as a unitless ratio (like `2`), it's calculated relative to the element's own font-size, not the parent's.

So `root>big` inherits the ratio `2` and applies it to its own font-size: `2 × 32px = 64px`.

This matches the browser data showing `root>big` with `line-height: 64px` and `height: 64px`.

ANSWER: 64
