## Model reply

Looking at the bounding boxes and viewport:

**Viewport:** 500×300

**root>lower:** Positioned at y=700 with height=40 (spans y=700–740). The root container is 900px tall, so vertical scrolling can bring this element into view.

**root>sideways:** Positioned at y=40 with **width=900px**. The viewport is only 500px wide, so this element extends 400px beyond the right edge. Ordinary scrolling on web pages is vertical—horizontal scrolling is not "ordinary."

The screenshot confirms this: the text "runs off sideways" is visibly cut off at the edge. Vertical scrolling cannot bring the overflowed horizontal portion into view.

ANSWER: root>sideways
