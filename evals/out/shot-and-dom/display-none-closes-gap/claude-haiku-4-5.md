## Model reply

Looking at the bounding rectangle data:

- `root>first` has `y=0` and `height=40`, so it occupies y=0 to y=40
- `root>third` has `y=40` and `height=40`, so it occupies y=40 to y=80
- `root>gone` has `display: none`, which removes it from the document flow entirely

Since `root>third` starts at y=40 (immediately where `root>first` ends), there is **no gap** between them. The `root>gone` element, despite having a height of 40px in its style, does not occupy any space in the layout because `display: none` removes it from the document flow completely. The third element moved up to fill the space where the gone element would have been.

ANSWER: NO
