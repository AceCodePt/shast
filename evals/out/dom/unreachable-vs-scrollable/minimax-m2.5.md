## Model reply

The `root` container is 500×900, taller than the 300px viewport, so vertical scrolling can reach the bottom of `root`. `root>lower` sits at y=700–740, well within that scrollable range, so scrolling down brings it into view.

`root>sideways`, however, is 900px wide while both `root` and the viewport are only 500px wide. Its overflow is horizontal (extending past the right edge), not vertical, and ordinary vertical scrolling won't reveal the clipped horizontal portion.

ANSWER: root>sideways
