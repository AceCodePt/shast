## Model reply

Looking at the browser's rendering data:

- `root>outer>filler` has a height of **60px**
- `root>outer>tall` has a height of **19.1875px**

Even though `root>outer>tall` declares `height: 100%`, the percentage height cannot resolve properly because its parent (`root>outer`) has no explicitly set height—it shrinks to fit its content. This causes the 100% to effectively compute to `auto`, leaving `tall` at its natural content height (just enough for the text "b").

ANSWER: NO
